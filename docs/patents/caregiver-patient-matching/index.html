Caregiver–patient matching

2024 · Granted patent · KR 10-2635315 · Co-inventor

Patent record

What the patent covers

The patent describes how to train a patient–caregiver matching model and use it to recommend candidates. The method draws on patient and caregiver information, location, and historical satisfaction data.

My role

I participated in the related research project and am a co-inventor on the granted patent. The project page explains the workflow and shows how the candidate shortlist feeds the recommendation.
