Method for training caregiver-patient matching model that infers matching information between caregiver and patient and matching caregiver-patient using caregiver-patient matching model

2024 · Granted patent · KR 10-2635315 · Co-inventor

Patent record

How location enters training

The patent compares the patient’s hospital location and the caregiver’s area of residence. It describes filtering pairs below a minimum match score or keeping the score as a feature.

How the method recommends a caregiver

A classifier estimates the probabilities of satisfaction and dissatisfaction. A regression model predicts a satisfaction rating. The recommendation claim uses both to score a potential pairing.
