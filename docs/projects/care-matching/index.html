Personalized information for patients and caregivers

Sep 2022–Jan 2023 · Research assistant, National Research Foundation of Korea

Patent record

Checking where care can take place

I was a research assistant on this National Research Foundation of Korea project. The team used the name of the patient’s hospital to look up its location, then compared it with the caregiver’s area of residence. That gave the matching model a way to account for where the two people could meet.

Using satisfaction from previous care

Previous patients’ satisfaction ratings supplied the target for training. The matching model estimates satisfaction for a new pairing, which the method uses to recommend a caregiver. I am a co-inventor on the resulting Korean patent, 10-2635315.
