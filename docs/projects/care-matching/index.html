Personalized information for patients and caregivers

Sep 2022–Jan 2023 · Research assistant, National Research Foundation of Korea

I contributed to the caregiver–patient matching project as a research assistant. Our team designed a model that uses patient and caregiver profiles, location, and past satisfaction to suggest matches. I am a co-inventor on the resulting patent.

Patent record

My role

I participated as a research assistant and am a co-inventor on the patent. The project addressed how to suggest a caregiver from multiple available candidates using information already collected during matching.

Building a shortlist

The patented approach uses information about the patient and caregiver, including location, to identify relevant candidates. Past service satisfaction is used to train a model to estimate how suitable a pairing may be.

Using the estimate

Those estimates help order the shortlisted candidates. The diagram traces the path from candidate selection to a recommendation, with the patent providing the full method.
