Care product recommendation system

Jul–Dec 2023 · Research assistant, National Research Foundation of Korea

Published patent application

What the team compared

I was a research assistant on this National Research Foundation of Korea project. The recommendation work combined patient intake fields and consultation notes with product information and reviews. The team considered two questions for each suggestion: whether the product met the patient’s needs and whether it could be used in that care setting.

From the project to the patent application

The work led to Korean patent application 10-2023-0185772, where I am named as a co-inventor. The published application describes how the system uses product similarity and checks the proposed items against the patient’s care setting.
