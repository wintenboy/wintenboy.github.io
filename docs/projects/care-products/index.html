Care product recommendation system

Jul–Dec 2023 · Research assistant, National Research Foundation of Korea

As a research assistant, I contributed to a recommender that uses patient needs, the care setting, and product information to suggest suitable supplies. I am a co-inventor on the associated published patent application.

Published patent application

My role

I participated as a research assistant in a National Research Foundation of Korea project on recommending care supplies. I am a co-inventor on the resulting published patent application.

How the team framed the recommendation

The system uses patient information, needs, and care setting as context for comparing products. The application describes combining structured fields with information from consultations and product reviews, then checking the suggested products against the environment in which they would be used.

What the system returns

The output is a list of products suited to a given patient and setting. The diagram describes the team's recommendation workflow as documented in the application.
