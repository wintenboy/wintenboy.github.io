Metal contamination detection in battery cathode materials

Jan–May 2025 · Researcher, EcoPro BM

Which lab result goes with a sensor trace?

Process measurements are timestamped. The lab’s ICP measurements are tied to production lots. I worked on matching the two so that each training record paired a sensor trace from the line with the lab result for the corresponding material.

Estimating the ICP measurement

I worked on features for the virtual metrology model that capture changes over time and interactions among process measurements. We used the QCP sensor data to estimate metal concentrations measured by ICP. The estimate was meant to help the team investigate a possible source of contamination.

A separate equipment health index

I also helped design an equipment health index. We looked at how it changed over time and whether the available signals could give enough advance notice of a change in equipment condition.
