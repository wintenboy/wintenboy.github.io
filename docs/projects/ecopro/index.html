Metal contamination detection in battery cathode materials

Jan–May 2025 · Researcher, EcoPro BM

I examined production sensor readings and lab assays for metal contamination. I mapped how the two sources could be matched by production lot to prepare labeled examples for a detection model.

Aligning measurements

Lab assays and process sensor readings arrive through different workflows. I checked how to align them by production lot so that a set of process measurements could be paired with the assay result it was meant to predict.

Developing the virtual metrology model

I worked on features that capture interactions among process variables and patterns over time. The model used QCP process measurements to estimate ICP metal concentration measurements. The goal was to give the team an earlier signal of potential contamination while they waited for lab results.

Monitoring the equipment

I also helped design an equipment health index and a prognosability measure. These were separate from the contamination model. They summarized equipment condition and helped assess whether the available signals could support early warning.
