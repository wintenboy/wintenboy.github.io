Metal contamination detection in battery cathode materials

Jan–May 2025 · Researcher, EcoPro BM

Matching sensor and lab records

The sensor data came as timestamped readings. The lab reported ICP results by production lot. I worked on identifying which stretch of sensor data belonged to each tested lot, so those records could be paired for training.

Estimating metal concentration

I worked on features that track changes in sensor readings and how different process variables move together. The virtual metrology model estimated ICP metal concentrations from QCP sensor readings. Those estimates could help the team investigate where contamination arose.

Equipment health index

I also worked on an equipment health index and a criterion for judging whether the sensor signals could give early warning of a change in equipment condition.
