Predicting plate-tip warping during rolling

Dec 2024–Nov 2025 · Researcher, POSCO

Measuring the direction of the bend

I worked on selecting frames, segmenting the plate with SAM2, and tracing its lower edge. The entry and exit cameras look at the plate from different directions. We flipped the entry images to align them and kept the sign of the curvature to distinguish an upward bend from a downward one.

Two passes ahead

Entry and exit images came from different collection dates, so consecutive passes of the same plate were hard to pair. We matched frames from the same side of the line, two passes apart. I helped build the support vector regression model using the image-derived warping index and process measurements such as rolling speed and plate thickness.
