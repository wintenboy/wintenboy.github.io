Predicting steel plate curvature after rolling

Dec 2024–Nov 2025 · Researcher, POSCO

I worked on selecting usable frames from rolling-line images, segmenting the plate, and extracting its lower edge. We used the outline to measure curvature and predict whether the plate tip would bend up or down in a later pass.

From rolling-line frames to a measure of curvature

I helped select usable frames, build the segmentation workflow, and extract the plate contours. The work included mask cleanup, edge extraction, and curve fitting. Once we had a reliable outline, we could use it to quantify how much the plate tip bent upward or downward.

Forecasting a later pass

We paired the image-derived warping index with measurements from roughing and finishing passes. I helped develop a support vector regression model to predict warping two passes ahead. Current warping, rolling speed, and plate thickness were among the process features we examined.

What the pipeline made possible

The project connected visual annotation and segmentation to an interpretable process quantity and a subsequent regression task. The project figure shows those stages in order.
