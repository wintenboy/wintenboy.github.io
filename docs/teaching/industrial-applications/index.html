ML & Industrial Applications (IIE3104-01)

Fall 2025 · Teaching assistant, Yonsei University

A semiconductor classification assignment

I supported a final assignment that asked students to classify normal and defective wafers from manufacturing sensor readings. Students justified their preprocessing and feature choices, then compared tree-based and boosting models using a validation plan.

From analysis to submission

Students submitted code, a report, and predictions in a specified CSV format. The evaluation combined test F1 with the reasoning in the report. My work included preparing the assignment and grading guidance.
