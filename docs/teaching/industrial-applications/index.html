ML & Industrial Applications (IIE3104-01)

Fall 2025 · Teaching assistant, Yonsei University

Five models on the same wafer data

I helped prepare and grade a final assignment that used process sensor readings to classify wafers as normal or defective. Students built Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, LightGBM, XGBoost, and CatBoost models. They submitted the predictions together in a five-column CSV.

Model comparison and reasoning

F1 on the held-out test set determined the model ranking portion of the grade. Students used the report to explain what they kept, how they handled the inputs, and how they chose and tuned the models.
