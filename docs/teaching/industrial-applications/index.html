Machine Learning and Industrial Applications (IIE3104-01)

Fall 2025 · Teaching assistant, Yonsei University

Wafer inspection assignment

I helped prepare and grade a final assignment on detecting defective wafers from process sensors. The assignment required Random Forest, Gradient Boosting, LightGBM, XGBoost, and CatBoost. Students submitted one column of predictions per model.

Grading

We scored test predictions using F1. In the reports I checked which variables students kept and how they validated and tuned their models.
